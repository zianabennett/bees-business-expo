# Run of show

## Time conventions

T is the event day. E is the public opening time on that day. Dates and times below are relative planning assumptions for the [portfolio scenario](../README.md), allowing the operating sequence to be reused without implying an actual historical schedule. The generated report captures the T-6 review.

## Preparation schedule

| When | Owner | Activity | Completion gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-21 | Event and finance leads | Confirm venue capacity and spending envelope | Booth inventory and category plan are recorded |
| T-14 | Outreach lead | Review prospective participants and outstanding invitations | Every invitation has a current status and owner |
| T-10 | Vendor coordinator | Collect setup requirements | Required information is recorded for confirmed vendors |
| T-8 | Event and outreach leads | Resolve participation decisions | Confirmed demand fits available capacity |
| T-7 | Logistics lead | Review all requested power arrangements | Each request has an approved arrangement or an active resolution plan |
| T-6 | Event team | Run the readiness and budget review | Prioritized exceptions have owners; new cost exposure is visible |
| T-5 | Logistics lead | Finalize booth assignments | Every confirmed vendor has a unique compatible booth |
| T-3 | Finance lead | Recheck remaining cost estimates | Forecast includes unpaid commitments and remaining work |
| T-2 | Vendor coordinator | Confirm arrivals and distribute final logistics | Arrival windows are recorded and vendors have current instructions |
| T-1 | Event lead | Review unresolved exceptions | The team understands which items remain open and who must resolve them |

The T-6 snapshot contains missed earlier gates. Those dates remain visible as overdue work instead of being silently moved forward. A missed deadline triggers follow-up and reassessment; it does not automatically cancel participation.

## Event-day sequence

| Time | Owner | Activity | Check before proceeding |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-120 minutes | Logistics lead | Review the venue and booth layout | Assigned spaces match the current plan |
| E-90 minutes | Vendor coordinator | Begin vendor arrivals | Arrival record identifies the vendor and assigned location |
| E-60 minutes | Logistics lead | Review setup exceptions | Power and placement concerns have an owner and feasible resolution |
| E-30 minutes | Event lead | Conduct opening readiness review | Open issues are understood; public areas and vendor locations are ready for the planned opening |
| E | Event team | Open the event | Team members know escalation contacts and current responsibilities |
| During event | Event lead | Review issues and changes | Every unresolved operational issue has an owner and next action |
| At close | Logistics lead | Coordinate departure and venue handoff | Departures and outstanding venue items are recorded |

## Closeout

At T+1, the finance lead records new invoices and moves settled commitments into the paid-cost state. This updates cash status without counting an expense twice. At T+3, the event team reviews unresolved costs and records lessons while the details remain available.

The retrospective should compare planned and observed processes, identify the causes of material exceptions, and assign any follow-up. Sales, attendance, and vendor satisfaction should be reported only if the event actually collected those measures; they cannot be inferred from the readiness model.
