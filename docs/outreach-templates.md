# Business Outreach Communication Templates

## How To Use These Samples

These new, AI-assisted retrospective templates accompany the [outreach playbook](outreach-playbook.md). They are based on my reported use of email, in-person outreach, phone calls and flyers for a UVA Black Business Expo. They are not original messages or evidence of historical campaign performance.

Bracketed fields are intentional event parameters. Replace them from the event lead's current brief before sending or printing. Dates, locations, fees and available places require actual event information; none are invented here. The samples use one response route per invitation and separate interest from a confirmed place.

## Shared Event Fields

| Field | Required Detail |
|---|---|
| `[Expo Name]` | Approved event name. |
| `[Organizer Name]` | Person responsible for the conversation. |
| `[Event Date And Hours]` | Date, public opening hours and time zone where relevant. |
| `[Location]` | Venue name and complete arrival address. |
| `[Arrival Window]` | Vendor arrival and setup period. |
| `[Participation Terms]` | Fee or no-fee status, provided equipment, and any selling restrictions. |
| `[Response Deadline]` | Date by which the owner should respond. |
| `[Reply Route]` | One monitored email address or approved sign-up form. |
| `[Next Update Date]` | Date promised for a pending decision or waitlist update. |

Personalize business references only after checking them. Remove an optional sentence when the relevant information is unavailable. Never turn an assumption about a business into a claim about its products, ownership or needs.

## Email Invitation

**Subject:** Invitation To Participate In [Expo Name]

Hi [First Name],

I'm [Organizer Name] with the University of Virginia's Black Economic Empowerment Society. We're inviting Black-owned and student businesses to take part in [Expo Name], an opportunity to introduce your business to campus customers and connect with other entrepreneurs.

Here are the participation details:

- **Event:** [Event Date And Hours]
- **Location:** [Location]
- **Vendor Setup:** [Arrival Window]
- **Participation Terms:** [Participation Terms]

Would you be interested in participating? Please reply by [Response Deadline] with your business name, preferred contact and any setup questions. I can help clarify the details before you decide. We will confirm your place after reviewing availability and participation requirements.

Thank you,

[Organizer Name]  
Black Economic Empowerment Society  
[Reply Route]

## Email Follow-Up

**Subject:** Following Up On [Expo Name]

Hi [First Name],

I'm following up on the invitation to [Expo Name] on [Event Date And Hours]. Are you interested, still considering it, or unable to participate?

If a question about setup or the participation terms is holding up your decision, please send it to me. A reply by [Response Deadline] will help us plan available places. If the event is not a fit, I will close the invitation and stop following up.

Thank you,

[Organizer Name]

## In-Person Introduction

“Hi, I'm [Organizer Name] with UVA's Black Economic Empowerment Society. We're organizing [Expo Name] to connect Black-owned and student businesses with campus customers. Would you be interested in hearing about participating?”

If the owner is interested:

“The event is [Event Date And Hours] at [Location]. The participation terms are [Participation Terms]. What would you need to know before deciding?”

After answering the question:

“May I send you the event details? Which contact route would you prefer? I'll note your question about [Specific Question] and get back to you by [Next Update Date].”

Record the agreed follow-up after the conversation. Ask for a decision only when the owner has the information needed to make one. An owner agreeing to receive details is interested, not confirmed.

## Phone Conversation Guide

**Opening**

“Hello, this is [Organizer Name] with UVA's Black Economic Empowerment Society. I'm calling about [Expo Name]. Is this a convenient time for a brief conversation?”

**Reason For Calling**

“We're inviting businesses to meet campus customers at [Expo Name]. I wanted to check whether you received the event details and whether there is a question I can help answer.”

**Decision Prompt**

“Based on those details, are you interested in participating, still considering it, or unavailable?”

**Close With A Specific Next Step**

“I'll [Agreed Action] by [Next Update Date]. You can reach me at [Reply Route]. Thank you for your time.”

If the owner is unavailable, ask for a suitable time or offer to send the details through their preferred route. If nobody answers, record a call attempt. Do not record it as a response or change the participation stage.

## Flyer Copy

### Bring Your Business To [Expo Name]

Meet campus customers. Introduce your work to the UVA community.

The Black Economic Empowerment Society invites Black-owned and student businesses to express interest in participating.

**When:** [Event Date And Hours]  
**Where:** [Location]  
**Participation Terms:** [Participation Terms]  
**Respond By:** [Response Deadline]

**Interested? Contact [Reply Route].**

Participation is subject to available space and the event's stated requirements. A place is confirmed when the event team sends a confirmation.

Keep the response route large enough to read. If using a QR code, generate it from the approved response URL and print the same route in readable text. Check the destination before distributing the flyer. Do not use a demonstration code or an unmonitored inbox.

## Interested Business Reply

**Subject:** Next Steps For [Expo Name]

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for your interest. Here are the current event details: [Event Date And Hours], [Location], and [Participation Terms].

Please confirm whether those arrangements work for you. Also let us know what you plan to bring, the space or equipment you need, and whether you require power. We will review your response against available places and setup arrangements, then update you by [Next Update Date].

Your interest is recorded. Your place will be confirmed in a separate message once that review is complete.

Thank you,

[Organizer Name]

## Participation Confirmation

Use this message only after the business has accepted the terms and the event lead has verified a place. Resolve material support questions before describing them as approved.

**Subject:** Your Place At [Expo Name] Is Confirmed

Hi [First Name],

We're pleased to confirm [Business Name]'s participation in [Expo Name].

- **Event:** [Event Date And Hours]
- **Location:** [Location]
- **Vendor Arrival:** [Arrival Window]
- **Agreed Participation Terms:** [Participation Terms]
- **Agreed Setup Arrangements:** [Approved Setup Details]

[Logistics Contact] will send the final placement and arrival instructions by [Next Update Date]. Please tell us promptly if your plans or setup requirements change. We will review any new request before confirming that we can accommodate it.

Thank you for participating,

[Organizer Name]

## Waitlist Update

**Subject:** Availability Update For [Expo Name]

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for your interest in [Expo Name]. The available places are currently committed. We can keep your business on the waitlist if you would like; a place is not yet confirmed.

We will update you by [Next Update Date], even if availability has not changed. Please reply if you would prefer to withdraw or if your availability changes. If a place opens, we will share the participation terms and ask for your decision before confirming it.

Thank you,

[Organizer Name]

## Closing An Unanswered Invitation

**Subject:** Closing Your Invitation To [Expo Name]

Hi [First Name],

The response deadline for [Expo Name] has passed, and we have not received a participation decision. We are closing this invitation for planning purposes.

If you are still interested, you may contact [Reply Route] to ask about current availability. We cannot guarantee a place. Thank you for considering the event.

[Organizer Name]

## Record The Outcome

After a reply or conversation, update the business's existing record with the outcome, current stage, unresolved question and next action owner. Preserve earlier contact history. Do not count a follow-up as another recruited business or attribute a confirmation to one channel without a clear reporting rule.

For confirmed businesses, continue with the [operations brief](operations-brief.md) and [run of show](run-of-show.md). Those documents use synthetic records and relative planning dates; they are supporting portfolio exercises rather than historical event logs.
